Base scenario `scenarios/default_72h.json` (digest `be2e728dadd833ab…`), engine 0.3.0. Ranking metric: overdue value × hours (lower = less disruption). ## 8. Caveats

- Results are properties of this synthetic network and calendar under the documented rules (docs/MODEL.md); they are not estimates for any real payment system.
- Overdue value × hours measures how much payment value is late and for how long. It is not economic loss.
- Outages that end near the 72 h horizon leave obligations unsettled; their delay is cut off at the horizon (right-censoring), which understates long-outage disruption for every intervention.
- The buffer and the credit line are untargeted; the backstop shows how much less a targeted allocation needs.
- The backup's benefit depends on its activation delay, its spare capacity and its independence from the failed service; all three are fixed here. Case 3 shows what happens when no independent backup exists.
